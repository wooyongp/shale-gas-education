Metalearners for Panel Data(Sketch)

Author

Wooyong Park

Published

October 7, 2025

Motivation

Currently, the grf package and causal forests do not support panel ata. However, researchers often use panel data to estimate causal effects, and as Athey and Imbens (2016) show, data-driven HTE estimation has much better qualities compared to testing researchers’ pre-defined hypotheses. Also, it is well established that R-learners specifically outperform in estimating HTEs nonparametrically(Robinson, 1988; Wager and Athey, 2018; Nie and Wager, 2020).

Notations

  • Y_{it}: outcome variable for individual i in period t.
  • D_{it}: treatment indicator for individual i in period t.
  • E_{i} = \min \{t: D_{it}=1\}: treatment adoption time for individual i.
  • X_i: individual-level covariates.
  • S_{it} = t - E_i: time of exposure to the treatment for individual i in period t

Metalearner

We want to estimate the following CATT:

CATT(x, s) = \mathbb{E}[Y_{it} | X_i = x, S_{it} = s] - \mathbb{E}[Y_{it} | X_i = x, S_{it} = 0]

which would, in some sense, give us event study plots for each x value.

R-Learner

DGP for the Panel Case

\begin{align} Y_{it} &= \sum_{s=1}^T \mathbb{I}(S_{it} = s)\tau(X_i,s) + g_0(X_i, \delta_t) + \varepsilon \\ D_{it} &= m_o(X_i) + \nu \end{align}

where \varepsilon and \nu satisfies \mathbb{E}[\varepsilon |D, X] = 0 and \mathbb{E}[\nu | X] = 0.

(1) implies that for untreated individuals and periods,

Y_{it} - Y_{it'} = g_0(X_i, \delta_t) - g_0(X_i, \delta_t') \qquad \forall t \neq t'

i.e., we can allow the trends to vary.

We can interpret the model as follows:

\begin{align} l_0(X) &:= m_o(X) &= \mathbb{E}[D | X] \quad (\text{Propensity Score}) \end{align}

Staggered Treatment Assumptions

As Sun and Abraham (2020) does, we also need the constant dynamic treatment effect assumption:

\begin{align} E[Y_{i,e+s} - Y_{i, e+s}^\infty \mid E_i = e] = \alpha_s \quad \forall e \in \{1, \ldots, T, \infty\} \end{align}

where

  • E_i = \min \{t: D_{i,t}=1\} is the first period that the individual i is treated.
  • D_{i,t} is the treatment indicator for individual i in period t.

LongBet Setup

\begin{align} Y_{it} &= Y_{it}(0) + \sum_{s=1}^T \mathbb{1}(t = e_i+S)\bigl(Y_{it}(S) - Y(0) \bigr)\\ Y_{it}(S) &\perp <D_{i1}, D_{i2}, \dots, D_{iT}> | X_i \qquad \forall S=0, 1, \dots T \end{align}

We further assume that each individual is iid, and that overlap is satisfied.

Treatment Effects

\begin{align} \tau_t(x, S) &:= \mathbb{E}[Y_{it}(S) - Y_{it}(0) | X_i = x, S]\\ &=\mathbb{E}[Y_{it} | X_i = x, S] - \mathbb{E}[Y_{it} | X_i = x, S=0] \end{align}
